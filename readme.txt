The following Mathematica files are implementations of the Habegger-Lin algorithm which determines whether given two links are link-homotopic for 4- and 5-component links, respectively.
Habegger-Lin_algorithm_4-comp.nb
Habegger-Lin_algorithm_5-comp.nb

For the details, we refer to the papers:
Y. Kotorii and A. Mizusawa, "Implementation of the Habegger–Lin decision algorithm", preprint, arXiv:math/2605.04433
Y. Kotorii and A. Mizusawa, "Clasper Presentations of Habegger–Lin’s Action on String Links", Experimental Mathematics 34 (2025), no. 4, 623–667.
